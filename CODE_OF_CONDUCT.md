# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks are not acceptable in any project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, or community chats.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately at **YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider harmful.
